SubTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Privacy at a glance: SubTracker is designed to help you track subscriptions while minimizing the personal information collected by the App. We do not sell your personal information. Subscription details you enter are intended to remain on your device unless a future feature specifically states otherwise.
Important for the developer: This policy is a stronger, more complete version of the policy you provided, but it is only safe to publish if it accurately describes the App's actual code, SDKs, permissions, databases, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, authentication, payment processing, and data flows. 1. Who We Are

SubTracker ("SubTracker," "the App," "we," "us," or "our") is developed and operated by Park Min Jung ("Developer").

Privacy questions, requests, and concerns may be submitted to: flameman506@gmail.com

2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information SubTracker collects, what information may be stored on your device or processed by service providers, why information is used, when information may be disclosed, how information is protected, and the choices available to you.

This Policy applies to information processed through the SubTracker mobile application and related services controlled by the Developer. It does not govern third-party websites, applications, or services that you may access through SubTracker. Those services have their own privacy practices.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, SubTracker may process:

  • Email address used for account creation, authentication, support, or account recovery.
  • Subscription names or merchant names that you choose to enter.
  • Subscription prices or estimated costs you choose to enter.
  • Billing frequency, billing cycle, category, renewal date, and related subscription information you choose to enter.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting support.

3.2 Authentication Information

If account authentication is enabled, authentication is handled through Google Firebase Authentication. SubTracker does not intentionally store passwords in plain text. The Developer does not receive your password through Firebase Authentication.

3.3 Purchase and Premium Information

If you purchase a Premium feature or subscription through Google Play, the purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. SubTracker may receive information necessary to determine whether you have an active entitlement, such as purchase or entitlement status. SubTracker does not intentionally collect or store your full payment card number through the App.

3.4 Device and Technical Information

Depending on the version of the App and the third-party SDKs actually installed, SubTracker may process limited technical information such as operating-system version, App version, device type, diagnostic information, or crash information. Any such collection must be reflected in the App's Google Play Data Safety disclosure.

3.5 Information We Do Not Intentionally Request

SubTracker is not designed to require government identification, Social Security numbers, passport numbers, driver's-license numbers, or similar identity documents. Please do not enter such information into subscription fields or support messages unless specifically requested for a lawful purpose.

4. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Subscription information entered into the App is intended to be stored locally on your device's local database. Local storage may persist until you delete the information, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, subject to the operating system and the actual implementation of the App.

If a future version of SubTracker synchronizes subscription information with a server, cloud database, backup service, or account, this Policy will be updated before or when that functionality is introduced.

5. How We Use Information

We use information only for legitimate purposes related to operating and improving SubTracker, including:

  • Creating and maintaining user accounts when account features are enabled.
  • Authenticating users and supporting password/account recovery.
  • Providing subscription tracking, reminders, calculations, categories, and other requested features.
  • Verifying Premium purchases and entitlements.
  • Responding to support requests and user communications.
  • Protecting the App, users, and services from fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security threats.
  • Diagnosing crashes, bugs, reliability problems, and technical issues when diagnostic tools are enabled.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations and responding to lawful requests.
  • Improving the App when the information used for that purpose is permitted by applicable law and consistent with this Policy.

6. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal information for money.
  • We do not rent your personal information to third parties for their own marketing.
  • We do not intentionally request Social Security numbers or government identification through normal App functionality.
  • We do not intentionally collect payment-card numbers through SubTracker's subscription-tracking interface.
  • We do not use subscription information for unrelated purposes that are not disclosed in this Policy.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information handled by SubTracker. Depending on the feature, these measures may include access controls, secure authentication, encrypted network transmission, secure platform storage, least-privilege access, dependency updates, and monitoring for security issues.

No application, database, device, network, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Accordingly, we cannot promise absolute security. If we become aware of a security incident affecting personal information, we will evaluate it and take steps required by applicable law, which may include notifying affected users or authorities.

10. Data Minimization and Privacy by Design

SubTracker is designed around data minimization: information that is not needed for a feature should not be requested or retained. We encourage users not to enter unnecessary sensitive information into subscription fields or support communications.

11. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, to provide the service, resolve disputes, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or enforce applicable agreements.

Locally stored subscription data generally remains on the device until the user deletes it, clears the App's data, or uninstalls the App. Account information maintained by authentication providers may remain until the account is deleted, subject to legally required retention or limited technical backup periods.

12. Account Deletion and Data Deletion

If SubTracker provides account creation, users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information by contacting flameman506@gmail.com.

Account deletion requests will be handled within a reasonable period and in accordance with applicable law. Where information must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or dispute purposes, we may retain only the information reasonably necessary for that purpose for the applicable retention period.

Deleting the App from your device can remove locally stored subscription information, but uninstalling an App does not necessarily delete an online account or information held by a third-party service. Account deletion should therefore be requested separately when an account exists.
